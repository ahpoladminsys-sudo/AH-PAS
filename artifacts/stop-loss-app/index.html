Stop-Loss Contract Basis Reference

Contract bases are written Incurred / Paid — the first number is the window in which a claim must be incurred, the second window in which it must be paid.

BasisContract TypeIncurred WindowPaid WindowWhat It Covers
12/12Standard / Initial Contract01/01/2025 – 12/31/202501/01/2025 – 12/31/2025Covers care both received and paid inside the active policy year.
24/12Run-In Contract01/01/2024 – 12/31/202501/01/2025 – 12/31/2025Picks up claims incurred in the prior 12 months when paid during the current year.
12/15Run-Out Contract01/01/2025 – 12/31/202501/01/2025 – 03/31/2026Protects against claim lag by giving providers three extra months to bill.
15/12Terminal Run-In10/01/2024 – 12/31/202501/01/2025 – 12/31/2025Picks up late-arriving claims incurred in the final quarter of the prior year.
