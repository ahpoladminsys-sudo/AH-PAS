# Card family

**Source evidence:** `artifacts/stop-loss-app/index.html`, shared `.card`,
`.card-header`, `.card-body`, `.kpi-card`, `.opp-detail-card`, and report
surface rules.

## Contract

- Use a white or dark elevated surface with a 1px border and a compact 8–11px
  radius.
- Keep card headers short and task-oriented; use serif or semibold display
  treatment for section titles.
- KPI cards may use a 3px navy, teal, gold, or red edge to signal category or
  risk without adding visual noise.
- Keep evidence, decision, and supporting detail in separate cards when a
  workflow requires review.
